# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to translate priorities into buildable scope
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery plans, dependencies, and timelines
- Partner with the Tech Lead / Architect on design decisions and engineering quality
- Collaborate with QA Lead / Test Engineer to validate acceptance criteria and release readiness

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with Project Managers on scope, sequencing, and external communications
- Work with Developers and Tech Lead / Architect on feasibility and trade-offs
- Partner with UX / Design Lead to validate user-centered requirements
- Coordinate with Product Operations / Program Manager for milestone planning and cross-project dependencies

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to align delivery schedules with roadmap priorities
- Work with Engineering Manager on staffing, capacity, and execution health
- Coordinate with QA Lead / Test Engineer to ensure release gates are clear and tracked
- Escalate dependencies and risks through Product Operations / Program Manager when needed

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead the health and performance of the delivery team. They focus on execution quality, team capacity, and technical delivery consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, staffing, and workload balance
- Support developer growth, coaching, and performance expectations
- Own engineering delivery health and operational efficiency
- Track technical debt and prioritize remediation work
- Partner with leadership on resourcing and execution trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver predictable execution and strong engineering quality
- Maintain a healthy, sustainable team operating model
- Improve throughput without sacrificing reliability or quality

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering team syncs
- Capacity and staffing discussions with PM and leadership
- Technical dependency and delivery risk updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on planning assumptions, dependencies, and delivery risk
- Supports Developers on work prioritization, mentoring, and process clarity
- Aligns with Tech Lead / Architect on architectural trade-offs and implementation quality
- Collaborates with QA Lead / Test Engineer on quality gates and release readiness

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design quality, and long-term system integrity. They help teams implement solutions that are maintainable, scalable, and aligned with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review major design decisions and implementation trade-offs
- Identify scalability, reliability, and maintainability concerns
- Support technical estimation and risk identification
- Own design documentation for critical systems and integrations

### Goals
- Maintain system quality and architectural coherence
- Reduce avoidable technical rework and integration risk
- Enable sustainable product growth

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical dependency planning
- Critical decision logs and design documentation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers and UX / Design Lead to evaluate feasibility and user impact
- Supports Developers with implementation guidance and design reviews
- Helps Project Managers understand technical risks and dependencies
- Coordinates with Security Engineer / Security Reviewer and SRE / DevOps Engineer on secure and resilient implementation

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX / Design Leads ensure that products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They transform user problems into clear product experiences and interaction patterns.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research and discovery efforts
- Define interaction flows, wireframes, and user experience standards
- Work with accessibility and design systems to improve usability
- Collaborate on prototype and validation activities
- Translate customer pain points into actionable product requirements

### Goals
- Improve usability, customer satisfaction, and product adoption
- Create consistent, accessible experiences across workflows
- Ensure product decisions are informed by user feedback

### Typical Communication
- Product workshops and requirement reviews
- Design critiques and validation sessions
- Feedback loops with PM, stakeholders, and developers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to refine value propositions and acceptance criteria
- Works with Developers to ensure proper implementation of UX decisions
- Collaborates with QA Lead / Test Engineer to validate usability and acceptance outcomes
- Helps Project Managers communicate design milestones and dependency impacts

---

## QA Lead / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Engineers define quality expectations and verify that work meets stated requirements before release. They help reduce defects, improve confidence, and support smooth delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, release checks, and quality gates
- Design test plans for unit, integration, and end-to-end scenarios
- Lead automation and regression coverage efforts
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify defect risks
- Support production readiness reviews

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce release risk
- Catch issues earlier in the lifecycle
- Increase confidence in releases and critical workflows

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and test review sessions
- Acceptance criteria validation with Product Managers and Developers
- Release readiness and bug triage discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on expected outcomes and acceptance criteria
- Works with Developers to confirm coverage and defect remediation
- Supports Project Managers in release confidence and milestone readiness
- Coordinates with Security Engineer / Security Reviewer on security and compliance-related validation

---

## Product Operations / Program Manager

### Role Summary
Product Operations / Program Managers support coordinated execution across project streams, releases, and stakeholder communications. They reduce planning friction and improve operational consistency.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate cross-project dependencies and sequencing
- Maintain milestone and release calendars
- Track program health, risks, and progress indicators
- Support stakeholder communications and status updates
- Facilitate process consistency across teams and initiatives

### Goals
- Improve operating rhythm and coordination
- Reduce cross-project bottlenecks and unclear priorities
- Ensure work moves forward with clear accountability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly portfolio or program review meetings
- Cross-team dependency updates
- Release and stakeholder communication summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to manage schedules and communication cadence
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap sequencing and stakeholder needs
- Supports Engineering Manager and QA Lead / Test Engineer with release readiness tracking
- Coordinates escalation paths when dependencies span multiple teams

---

## Security Engineer / Security Reviewer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers and Security Reviewers identify and mitigate security risks throughout the product lifecycle. They help teams build secure systems without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and implementations for security issues
- Perform threat modeling and vulnerability triage
- Validate security requirements and controls
- Support secure deployment and remediation planning
- Collaborate on incident response and secure release criteria

### Goals
- Reduce product and customer exposure to security risk
- Improve secure-by-default development practices
- Maintain confidence in production systems

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and remediation planning
- Risk and vulnerability discussions with Engineers and leadership
- Security sign-off requirements for releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Reviews work with Developers and Tech Lead / Architect to address design and implementation risks
- Partners with QA Lead / Test Engineer on validation of security-critical scenarios
- Coordinates with SRE / DevOps Engineer on secure deployments and monitoring
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers on compliance and risk communication needs

---

## Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
SREs and DevOps Engineers keep systems reliable, observable, and deployable. They enable smooth delivery to production and help teams recover quickly from operational issues.

### Responsibilities
- Manage deployment pipelines and environment configuration
- Own observability, alerting, and service health monitoring
- Support incident response and operational runbooks
- Improve release automation and rollback readiness
- Help teams design for resiliency and recovery

### Goals
- Maintain system availability and fast recovery
- Improve deployment safety and operational visibility
- Reduce production incidents and mean time to recovery

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Operational incident updates and postmortems
- Deployment health and monitoring dashboards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Tech Lead / Architect on deployability and resilience requirements
- Partners with QA Lead / Test Engineer for release validation and smoke testing
- Supports Project Managers and Product Managers with release planning and risk communication
- Coordinates with Security Engineer / Security Reviewer on secure infrastructure and incident handling

---

## Data Analyst / Data Engineer

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Data Engineers provide measurement, instrumentation, and analysis to help teams understand product outcomes and improve decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Define analytics requirements and instrumentation plans
- Build dashboards and reporting for key metrics
- Support experiment design and interpretation
- Maintain data pipelines and data quality checks
- Measure business and product outcomes against success metrics

### Goals
- Improve insight into product performance and customer value
- Support evidence-based decision-making
- Make success metrics visible and actionable

### Typical Communication
- Metrics planning and reporting
- Product performance reviews
- Experiment and business outcome analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to define success metrics and product KPIs
- Supports Developers and QA Lead / Test Engineer with feature instrumentation and validation
- Helps Project Managers and Product Operations / Program Manager track delivery outcomes
- Provides evidence to inform roadmap decisions and post-release assessments

---

## Customer Success / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support representatives translate customer realities into actionable feedback and operational insights. They help teams understand pain points and recurring issues.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback and support trends
- Prioritize recurring issues and customer-impacting bugs
- Share adoption, usability, and workflow insights
- Support stakeholder communication around customer pain points
- Help validate release impact and user satisfaction

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and retention
- Reduce friction in customer-facing workflows
- Surface product issues early and clearly

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries
- Bug triage and issue escalation
- Release impact and customer adoption reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds Product Managers with customer insight and emerging needs
- Works with QA Lead / Test Engineer on defect prioritization and validation
- Coordinates with Project Managers on escalation and communication timing
- Informs Developers and Tech Lead / Architect about real-world usage pain points

---

## Legal / Compliance Contact

### Role Summary
Legal and Compliance contacts review obligations related to privacy, contractual requirements, regulatory standards, and policy constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Review product and data handling practices for compliance
- Assess legal risk for contracts, releases, and integrations
- Support privacy, security, and governance requirements
- Clarify obligations for external partnerships and customer commitments

### Goals
- Reduce legal and regulatory risk
- Ensure product decisions remain compliant and defensible
- Support responsible delivery and customer trust

### Typical Communication
- Contract and compliance review cycles
- Legal risk assessments and policy questions
- Release gating for regulated or sensitive scenarios

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers on compliance-sensitive milestones
- Works with Security Engineer / Security Reviewer on privacy and risk standards
- Provides guidance to Developers and Tech Lead / Architect for regulated implementation patterns
- Advises on release readiness when legal review is required
